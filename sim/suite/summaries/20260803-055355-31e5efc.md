## Measurement conventions

- **Output reference** (`output-voltage-tc`): Corner portion of the row only: process x temperature x supply. The row's 3-sigma untrimmed *mismatch* portion (mismatch MC, N>=300) is #13's `mc-untrimmed` bench; the two legs are judged together by the combined verdict below (`COMBINED_ACCURACY`, `sim/suite/combined.py`), which is the row's ratified basis. This line remains reported on its own so it stays visible which leg binds.
- **Temp coefficient (-40..125 degC)** (`output-voltage-tc`): Box method over an internal 1 degC-step -40..125 degC sweep (166 points per corner), not the three-point outer axis; TC = (Vmax - Vmin)/(V_27C * 165 degC) * 1e6.
- **PSRR** (`psrr-dc`): DC figure = the low-frequency asymptote at 1 Hz (an AC analysis cannot evaluate 0 Hz); 1 kHz is the band edge the row names. The other spot frequencies are the reference curve, not claims; 1 MHz is the table's stretch goal (> 30 dB), recorded not gated. Unloaded -- the row's load condition is open item A4.
- **Line regulation** (`line-regulation`): Box over a continuous 133-point supply sweep, (Vmax - Vmin)/0.66 V -- not the endpoint chord.
- **Supply / Output reference over supply** (`line-regulation`): The supply-tolerance aspect of the output-reference row, checked at every one of the 133 sweep points rather than only at the three nominal rails.
- **Quiescent current** (`iq`): Total supply current of the whole block at every PVT corner; the row binds at ff/125 degC/3.63 V. There is no separate startup branch to itemize yet -- when #11's branch lands inside bandgap_top it is counted by this same measurement, and #11's own itemization should be cross-referenced from the record then.
- **Startup** (`startup`): Verified by #11's transient bench and degenerate-state search. This suite wires that bench in by slug and reports its own overall verdict; it deliberately does NOT reimplement it.
