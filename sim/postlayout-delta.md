## Benches outside the per-corner tables above

The tables above cover the four spec-line benches that take a `--dut`. Two
further post-layout record sets belong to this pass and are compared here in
prose, because their benches are not part of `sim/suite/spec.py`'s gated index
and `postlayout_delta.py` therefore has no limits to align them against.

### Startup (#11's rows), post-extraction

`sim/startup/` and `sim/startup-state-search/` embed their own copy of the
circuit and declare no `dut` key: both insert a zero-volt ammeter *inside* the
`bandgap_startup` subcircuit instance, so `--dut` does not swap their circuit,
it `.include`s a second conflicting `bandgap_top` and the run dies on the
dangling `v.xtop.vsu_sense#branch` probe. An extracted netlist is flat and has
no such hierarchy to tap at all. #17 therefore adds
`sim/startup-extracted/` and `sim/startup-state-search-extracted/` — same
analyses, same checks, same settle definitions, hierarchical probes respelled
for a flat netlist — and both drop `iq_startup_branch_final_ua`, which cannot
be isolated without the hierarchy; `det_final_v` carries the disengagement
evidence instead.

**The degenerate zero-current state is still not a trap post-extraction**: at

## Analysis

### Headline

Three ratified spec lines FAIL on the extracted netlist: Output reference,
Temp coefficient, and Supply / Output reference over supply (which gates the
same `vref` window across the supply sweep). **All three already failed at
schematic level** — the extracted netlist misses them by a larger margin
(`vref` is 36.8–52.2 mV higher at every one of the 81 corners, worst-corner TC 1.7× worse) but introduces no new
failing line. PSRR, Line regulation, Quiescent current and Startup all still
pass post-layout.

Per CLAUDE.md's no-relaxation rule the widened misses are reported as FAIL and
escalated, not absorbed. Nothing in `spec/`, `sim/suite/spec.py` or any
`tb.json` limit was touched.

### The delta is one device-geometry divergence, not parasitics

With #86 fixed upstream, the **entire** DC-domain shift is attributable to a
single remaining place where the drawn layout does not implement the
schematic. It is invisible to DRC and LVS, because
`layout/lvs/make_reference.py` derives its reference from the drawn geometry
rather than from the schematic — so the layout is self-consistent and
`status: match` while still being a different circuit.

| Escalated as | Divergence | Schematic | As drawn | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [#87](https://github.com/2AMLogic/gf180-bandgap/issues/87) | `core.Q2` drawn as four parallel `pnp_05p00x05p00` units (floorplan §4.1's common-centroid choice) but modelled in the schematic as one `pnp_10p00x10p00`, whose `is` is 3.671× — not 4× — a unit's | ΔVBE 33.327 mV @ 27 °C (implied emitter-area ratio **3.628**) | ΔVBE 35.998 mV @ 27 °C (implied ratio **4.022**) | **+8.0 %** on the PTAT term |
| ~~[#86](https://github.com/2AMLogic/gf180-bandgap/issues/86)~~ | folded resistors drawn `(n−1)·W` short — **fixed on `main` by [#91](https://github.com/2AMLogic/gf180-bandgap/pull/91)**, and this evidence set is taken after it | `(R1+trim)/R2` = 15.118 nominal | same | **0** |

Measured by an `op` at `tt` / 27 °C / 3.30 V on the two DUT netlists
(`sim/dut/bandgap_top.spice` and `layout/netlist/bandgap_top_extracted.spice`),
where `vref = VBE3 + (R1+trim)/R2 · ΔVBE`:

| Term | Schematic | Extracted | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| `VBE1` = `v(sns1)` | 704.917 mV | 706.925 mV | +2.008 mV |
| `VBE2` = `v(core_e2)` | 671.591 mV | 670.927 mV | −0.664 mV |
| ΔVBE = `VBE1 − VBE2` | 33.327 mV | 35.998 mV | **+8.01 %** |
| `VBE3` = `v(core_e3)` | 704.917 mV | 706.925 mV | +2.008 mV |
| PTAT term = `vref − VBE3` | 509.371 mV | 550.232 mV | +40.86 mV |
| effective `(R1+trim)/R2` | **15.284** | **15.285** | **+0.007 %** |
| total supply current | 20.714 µA | 22.233 µA | +7.3 % |
| **measured `vref`** | **1.214288 V** | **1.257157 V** | **+42.87 mV** |

The effective resistor ratio is now identical to five significant figures on
both sides — that is the #91 fix, and it is why the whole 42.87 mV reduces to
one term:

- ΔVBE through the PTAT gain: `15.284 × (35.998 − 33.327)` = **+40.82 mV**
- `VBE3` rising with `ln I` (the same +8.0 % current, since `R2` is now the
  same on both sides): `VT · ln(1.0801)` = **+2.01 mV**
- residual left for parasitics and ratio drift: **+0.04 mV**

That residual is the whole point of the attribution: 99.9 % of the post-layout
`vref` error is #87, not layout parasitics.

The +8.0 % core current is also why `Iq` rises (worst corner 36.27 µA vs
34.01 µA against a 50 µA limit — headroom narrows from 32 % to 27 %). The
+8.0 % PTAT gain is why TC degrades: the core was curvature-balanced for the
schematic's ΔVBE, and an over-large ΔVBE over-compensates it, so `vref` climbs
across more of the −40…125 °C sweep instead of turning over inside it (worst
box TC 90.50 → 151.02 ppm/°C).

Line regulation moves the *other* way at the worst corner (0.0415 →
0.0388 mV/V) for the same reason — more core current means more loop gain and
lower output impedance. That is not evidence the layout is good; it is
downstream of the same #87 current error.

### What the extracted parasitics actually contributed

`klt extract --parasitics` emitted 82 R and 82 C (209.6 kΩ and 2483.0 fF
total). Their **topology** matters more than their magnitude: each net gets

```
R_<net>  <net>       <net>__par     <R_total>
C_<net>  <net>__par  <substrate>    <C_total>
```

so the resistance's only other terminal is that capacitor's own plate. It
carries no DC current and sits between no driver and any receiver. Two
consequences for reading this delta:

- **No DC-domain number here is a parasitic-resistance result.** IR drop on
  supply/ground/bias distribution — normally the first thing a post-layout
  re-run of a precision reference goes looking for — is structurally absent
  from this netlist, not merely small. Filed generically upstream as
  [klayout-tools#338](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/338).
  The +0.04 mV DC residual above is consistent with exactly that: there is no
  mechanism in this netlist for the extracted R to move a DC operating point.
- **The capacitance is real and does load the nets** (its isolating pole sits
  above 100 MHz for every net here), so it is what moves the AC-domain
  results. It shows up cleanly in `psrr-dc` as a *frequency-dependent* change
  that the DC-domain error cannot explain: at 1 Hz the mean rises (99.48 →
  99.86 dB), while at 1 kHz the mean falls (90.85 → 81.10 dB) and the best
  corner falls hardest (99.22 → 85.32 dB); by 10 kHz the mean is 74.72 →
  62.14 dB. That is added shunt capacitance rolling the supply-rejection pole
  in — the one unambiguously parasitic effect in this whole delta. Both
  *gated* figures (1 Hz, 1 kHz) stay above the 60 dB limit at every corner;
  the 10 kHz and above columns are recorded reference data, not spec lines
  (the ratified row is "> 60 dB DC–1 kHz").

So this pass substantiates the post-layout behaviour of the *drawn devices*
faithfully, and of the *drawn interconnect* only in its capacitive half. That
is a real limit on what "post-layout verified" can mean for this block today,
and it is stated here rather than left for a reader to infer.
