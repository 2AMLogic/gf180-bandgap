further post-layout record sets belong to this pass and are compared here in
prose, because their benches are not part of `sim/suite/spec.py`'s gated index

### Startup (#11's rows), post-extraction

`sim/startup/` and `sim/startup-state-search/` embed their own copy of the
circuit and declare no `dut` key: both insert a zero-volt ammeter *inside* the
`bandgap_startup` subcircuit instance, so `--dut` does not swap their circuit,
it `.include`s a second conflicting `bandgap_top` and the run dies on the
dangling `v.xtop.vsu_sense#branch` probe. An extracted netlist is flat and has
no such hierarchy to tap at all. #17 therefore adds
`sim/startup-extracted/` and `sim/startup-state-search-extracted/` — same
analyses, same checks, same settle definitions, hierarchical probes respelled
for a flat netlist — and both drop `iq_startup_branch_final_ua`, which cannot
be isolated without the hierarchy; `det_final_v` carries the disengagement
evidence instead.

**The degenerate zero-current state is still not a trap post-extraction**: at
every one of the 27 corners the block seeded exactly at the degenerate point
(`.ic` + `uic`, supply already valid) recovers to a genuine operating current,
and does so *faster* than the schematic-level netlist did. That is the
#17 Test Plan's "confirm the degenerate zero-current startup state (#11) is
re-checked post-extraction" item, and it passes.

Both times get shorter rather than longer, which is the opposite of the naive
expectation for added parasitic capacitance. It is the same root cause as
everything else in this document: the drawn resistors are short (#86), the
core current is ~28 % higher, and a faster-charging loop settles sooner. The
extracted ground capacitance is real but small against that.

### Monte Carlo mismatch (#13), post-extraction

The extraction models **do** support the mismatch analysis, and this was
confirmed rather than assumed: the mismatch `sim/mc-untrimmed/` exercises lives
in the gf180mcu device subcircuits (`fets_mm` / `bjt_mc`, gated by

The extracted netlist in fact carries *more* independent mismatch draws than

separate device instances: 81 discrete MOS fingers where the schematic has 25

N = 300, seed 20260801, nominal supply, −40/27/125 °C, four sensitivity groups.

|---|---|---|---|

| `mm_all` (claim-supporting) | 1.3481 % | 1.3475 % | **−0.05 %** |

## Analysis

### Headline

Three ratified spec lines FAIL on the extracted netlist: Output reference,
Temp coefficient, and Supply / Output reference over supply (which gates the
same `vref` window across the supply sweep). **All three already failed at
schematic level** — the extracted netlist misses them by a much larger margin
(`vref` ~85 mV higher at every corner, TC ~4× worse) but introduces no new
failing line. PSRR, Line regulation, Quiescent current and Startup all still
pass post-layout.

Per CLAUDE.md's no-relaxation rule the widened misses are reported as FAIL and
escalated, not absorbed. Nothing in `spec/`, `sim/suite/spec.py` or any
`tb.json` limit was touched.

### The delta is device geometry, not parasitics

The whole DC-domain shift is attributable to two places where the **drawn
layout does not implement the schematic**. Both are invisible to DRC and LVS,
because `layout/lvs/make_reference.py` derives its reference from the drawn
geometry rather than from the schematic — so the layout is self-consistent and
`status: match` while still being a different circuit.

| Escalated as | Divergence | Schematic | As drawn | Effect on the PTAT term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [#86](https://github.com/2AMLogic/gf180-bandgap/issues/86) | Folded resistors drawn `(n−1)·W − 2·IMPLANT_ENC` short: `plan.res_geometry` credits every serpentine fold with a full `pitch` of resistive length, but a fold's centreline contributes only `POLY_SP` | `R1` 460.702 µm, `R2` 36.342 µm; ratio `(R1+trim)/R2` = **15.118** | `R1` 407.08 µm (−11.6 %), `R2` 30.74 µm (−15.4 %); ratio **16.128** | **+6.7 %** |
| [#87](https://github.com/2AMLogic/gf180-bandgap/issues/87) | `core.Q2` drawn as four parallel `pnp_05p00x05p00` units (floorplan §4.1's common-centroid choice) but modelled in the schematic as one `pnp_10p00x10p00`, whose `is` is 3.671× — not 4× — a unit's | ΔVBE ratio **3.632**, ΔVBE 33.34 mV @ 27 °C | ΔVBE ratio **4.027**, ΔVBE 36.01 mV @ 27 °C | **+8.0 %** |

Reconciling those two against the measured `vref` at `tt_27c_3.30v`, where
`vref = VBE3 + (R1+trim)/R2 · ΔVBE`:

| Term | Schematic | Extracted |
|---|---|---|
| ΔVBE @ 27 °C | 33.34 mV | 36.01 mV |
| `(R1+trim)/R2` | 15.118 | 16.128 |
| PTAT term | 504.1 mV | 580.8 mV |
| core current `I = ΔVBE/R2` | 5.24 µA | 6.69 µA (+27.7 %) |
| `VBE3` (rises with `ln I`) | 710.3 mV | 716.6 mV |
| **predicted `vref`** | **1.2144 V** | **1.2974 V** |
| **measured `vref`** | **1.21429 V** | **1.29880 V** |

Both predictions land within ~1.5 mV of the measured value, which is what
makes the attribution safe: the shift is fully accounted for by the two
drawn-geometry divergences, with no residual left for parasitics to explain.

The +27.7 % core current is also why `Iq` rises (25.97 µA vs 20.71 µA at
`tt_27c_3.30v`, worst corner 42.43 µA vs 34.01 µA against a 50 µA limit — the
one passing line whose margin visibly narrowed, from 32 % to 15 % headroom).
The +15.5 % net PTAT gain is why TC blows out: the core was curvature-balanced
for a 15.118 ratio and is now heavily over-compensated, so `vref` climbs
monotonically across the whole −40…125 °C sweep instead of turning over inside
it.

Two passing lines moved in the *helpful* direction, both for the same reason —
a higher core current means more loop gain and lower output impedance:
`linereg_mv_per_v` improves (worst 0.0415 → 0.0286 mV/V) and the 1 Hz PSRR
asymptote improves (worst 73.93 → 76.53 dB). Neither is evidence the layout is
good; both are downstream of the same #86/#87 current error.

### What the extracted parasitics actually contributed

`klt extract --parasitics` emitted 82 R and 82 C (207.9 kΩ and 2466.8 fF
total). Their **topology** matters more than their magnitude: each net gets

```
R_<net>  <net>       <net>__par     <R_total>
C_<net>  <net>__par  <substrate>    <C_total>
```

so the resistance's only other terminal is that capacitor's own plate. It
carries no DC current and sits between no driver and any receiver. Two
consequences for reading this delta:

- **No DC-domain number here is a parasitic-resistance result.** IR drop on
  supply/ground/bias distribution — normally the first thing a post-layout
  re-run of a precision reference goes looking for — is structurally absent
  from this netlist, not merely small. Filed generically upstream as
  [klayout-tools#338](https://github.com/2AMLogic/klayout-tools/issues/338).
- **The capacitance is real and does load the nets** (its isolating pole sits
  above 100 MHz for every net here), so it is what moves the AC-domain
  results. It shows up cleanly in `psrr-dc` as a *frequency-dependent* change
  that the DC-domain error cannot explain: at 1 Hz the mean rises (99.48 →
  102.43 dB, tracking the higher loop gain), while at 1 kHz the mean falls
  (90.85 → 81.99 dB) and the best corner falls hardest (99.22 → 86.04 dB).
  That is added shunt capacitance rolling the supply-rejection pole in — the
  one unambiguously parasitic effect in this whole delta. Both figures stay
  above the 60 dB limit at every corner.

So this pass substantiates the post-layout behaviour of the *drawn devices*
faithfully, and of the *drawn interconnect* only in its capacitive half. That
is a real limit on what "post-layout verified" can mean for this block today,
and it is stated here rather than left for a reader to infer.
