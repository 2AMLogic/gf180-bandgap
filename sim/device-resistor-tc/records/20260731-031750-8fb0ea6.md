A VCR of exactly zero is a **model limitation, not a measurement**: the gf180mcu resistor subcircuits set `r_vc1 = r_vc2 = 0`, so voltage coefficient is not modelled in this PDK release. Silicon high-sheet poly does have a finite VCR; a design that relies on VCR being negligible cannot cite this simulation as evidence.
